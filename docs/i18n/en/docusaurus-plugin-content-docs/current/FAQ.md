# FAQ – Frequently asked questions

## On which platforms will the app be available?

- **Android (Play Store):** Yes, the app will initially be available in the Google Play Store.  
- **iOS (App Store):** We are currently unable to provide any information about the release for iOS. We are working on it and will keep you informed.

---

## How can I submit an achievement?

You can submit your ideas directly via the [Submit your own achievement](https://farin25.github.io/real-live-achievement/docs/Dein_Achievment) page.  
Please describe clearly:

1. Name of the achievement  
2. What exactly needs to be done to achieve it  
3. How it should be recorded or verified  

The more specific your information is, the easier it will be to implement.

---

## Do I need to create an account to use the app?

No, an account is **not required**.  
- If you use the app without an account, you can test it locally, but you will not have access to features such as **friends, rankings, or cloud sync**.  
- An account is required to use all features.

---

## How can I export or delete my data?

- **Data export:** Under EU law (GDPR), every user can export their stored data. To do this, you currently need to send us an email.  
- **Data deletion:** If you want to delete your account, you also need to send us an email. All personal data and achievements will then be removed.

---

## What privacy settings are available?

- **Private:** Only you can see your achievements.  
- **Friends only:** Only users who are your friends can see your achievements.  
- **Public:** All users can see your achievements.  

---
