# Submit your own achievement

## Design Mark Up with 

Do you have an idea for a new achievement?  
Then submit it here!

We review every submission and then decide whether and in what form the achievement will be included in the app.

---

## What should your achievement include?

Please make sure your suggestion clearly describes the following points:

### 1. Name of the achievement
- What is it called?
- Short, understandable, and motivating.

### 2. Description
- What exactly do you have to do?
- Are there any conditions or restrictions?

### 3. Recording/verification
- How should the achievement be verified?
  - Manual confirmation?
  - Time-based?
  - Age-based?
  - Location-based?
  - Later, possibly via fitness integration?
- Should it be awarded automatically or manually?

The clearer your suggestion is, the better we can implement it.

---

## Important notes

- There is no guarantee of publication.
- We reserve the right to modify or not implement suggestions.
- Inappropriate or illegal content will not be considered.
