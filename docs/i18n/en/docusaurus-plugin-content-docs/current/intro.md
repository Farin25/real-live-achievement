**Up Mark** is the achievement system for real life. Unlike in games, where you earn trophies by pressing buttons, **Up Mark** rewards you for actually going out and experiencing the world. Travel to new countries, climb mountain peaks, spend a night under the stars, or simply take a particularly long walk. Every achievement you unlock is something you've actually experienced. Connect with friends, see what they've unlocked, and discover new experiences you might never have tried otherwise.
